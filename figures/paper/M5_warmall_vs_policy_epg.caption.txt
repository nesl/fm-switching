M5_warmall_vs_policy_epg

CLAIM:
On EP-G, the warm-everything baseline warm-all-lru serves only 5% of dashcam opportunities within the 1 s deadline and 6% of SS recalls within 2 s, and its dashcam service latency grows from about 1 s early in the run to about 438 s late, because prefilling every batch saturates the engine. The selective policy hybrid-F-lhreq-noc3 serves 65% of dashcam within 1 s and 96% of SS within 2 s and holds a flat 0.8 s dashcam latency across the run. Warming indiscriminately is worse than the selective policy, which is the case for a policy rather than a fixed warm-all rule.
