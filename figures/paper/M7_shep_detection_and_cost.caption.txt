M7_shep_detection_and_cost

CLAIM:
At every episode condition the cloud 32B detects sudden hazards better than the device 4B: the mAP gap runs 10.0 to 16.2 pp and every 95 percent CI is above zero. Adding frames does not close the gap: the device 4B does not improve beyond the one-frame C1 condition (mAP 0.538) and falls at the two highest frame-count conditions C6 (0.478) and EP-G (0.466). The cost of episode context is paid on the cold path only: full-history EP-G context costs the device 1.88 s cold at first-token p50 but 0.29 s warm, close to the 0.24 s one-frame cost.
