E5_memory_sweep

CLAIM:
Under a shrinking KV pool, the pool-aware warming policy P3 holds first-token latency at about 0.56 s even at one-quarter pool, while the naive GPU-only warming policy P1 rises from 0.61 s to 1.87 s as its coverage collapses. The no-warming base is flat but high because it never warms. Memory pressure is handled by the policy, not by the pool size alone.
