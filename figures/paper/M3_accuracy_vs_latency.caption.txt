M3_accuracy_vs_latency

CLAIM:
Across tiers and representations, the richer full-history representation buys accuracy over the recent window (device 28% to 29%, cloud 33% to 37% on EVAL) but at a cold first-token cost that puts the device over the 3 s budget (device recent 3.32 s, device rich 6.63 s). Warming removes that cost: the device recent-window first token drops from 3.32 s cold to 0.21 s warm at the same accuracy, moving the device from over budget to well under it. A long-history representation can therefore be both accurate and on time only if its prefix is warm.
