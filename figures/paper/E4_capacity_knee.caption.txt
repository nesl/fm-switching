E4_capacity_knee

CLAIM:
The serving engine saturates at a sharp capacity knee between 4512 and 5192 offered uncached tokens per second. Clean cells below the knee hold first-token latency near 0.45 s, while a cell above it jumps to 36 s first-token p50. At 16 devices the clean policies sit below the knee; the knee is the per-engine capacity that bounds how large a fleet one engine can serve on time.
