M8_ss2_accuracy_gap_by_dataset

CLAIM:
On StudySS2, the cloud 32B beats the device 4B on both datasets. The gap is much larger on RefSpatial spatial reasoning, where cloud 37.1% beats device 25.1% by a paired 12.1 pp (95% CI [5.9, 18.3]), than on RealWorldQA general visual QA, where cloud 71.2% beats device 65.5% by 5.8 pp (95% CI [2.4, 9.2]). The value of escalating to the cloud therefore depends on the task.
