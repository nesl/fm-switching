M6_warmstate_kv_pool

CLAIM:
In the offline warm-state sim, a no-warming policy holds 78% on time at every pool size because it never uses the pool, while warming the right histories reaches 100 percent on time at the deployed pool. As the KV pool shrinks to one quarter, a pool-aware warming policy that spills to the host tier (P3) holds full warm coverage (0.95) and 100% on time, while a naive GPU-only warming policy (P1) loses coverage (0.00) and falls to 83% on time. The value of warm state depends on managing the pool, not just on turning warming on.
