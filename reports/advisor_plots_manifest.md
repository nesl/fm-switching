## E — ERQA Think-Tokens vs Correctness, Within Category

**Source file:** `results/erqa/study_k/study_k_trials.jsonl` (THINKING arm, 3 seeds = 1,200 trials)

**Computation:** Within each category, think-token counts are binned into quartiles (Q1–Q4) of that category's distribution only. Accuracy (fraction correct) and Wilson 95% CI computed per quartile. **Pooling across categories is not done** — doing so would manufacture a correlation from the category difficulty mix (harder categories produce both more tokens and lower accuracy).

|----------|---|---------|---|

| Task Reasoning | −0.321 | 0.001 | 114 |

| Multi-view Reasoning | +0.031 | 0.750 | 111 |
| Pointing | −0.118 | 0.240 | 102 |
| State Estimation | −0.002 | 0.976 | 165 |
| Trajectory Reasoning | −0.081 | 0.256 | 198 |

**Interpretation:** Negative r within Spatial Reasoning and Task Reasoning means harder-to-answer questions (where the model fails) generate more tokens, not fewer. The model spends more tokens when it is confused, not when it succeeds.

**Caption:** Within-category accuracy by think-token quartile for ERQA THINKING arm (n=1,200 across 3 seeds). Q1–Q4 are quartiles of each category's own token distribution — not pooled.
