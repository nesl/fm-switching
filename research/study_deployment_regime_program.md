Design principle: the policy is built for the space of plausible deployments, not tuned to the current testbed: each component is justified by the deployment regime where it acts and must do no harm elsewhere, not by whether the default setting reaches it. Builds: all switches default OFF; light checks per build only (switch off = byte-identical to the base; switch on changes at least one decision of its own kind on a dry replay). Deployment: one by one, one seed (s4), each step on its own regime PLUS a no-harm check; readouts = cbd (device credit 0.257732, D281, where no replayed answers), loss decomposition (wrong / rejected cold / late engine / late dispatch), D280 FAIR share; no verdict rules (the user decides). D240 holds (no stacking of memory pressure and network outage) until a D240 exception is granted (NOT granted). Outage network scope: correlated, per-device uplinks as committed, no shared-uplink modelling.
## THREE LINES PER BUILD (triggering deployment condition / mechanism / recoverable bound there, from committed evidence; every bound is an estimate from one seed s4 and a stated source)
**0b, correct booking.** Trigger: any regime where a session's prefix is EVICTED and the next piece re-prefills the full history: n32 3:11:2 (286 of 536 robot pieces, 2.22M tokens above piece size); halved memory. Mechanism: book the belief-implied uncached work (cold: full prefix + piece; else piece only) at the single booking point, so every consumer (budget, queued_uncached_ahead, D162 harm, release re-check) reads it. Bound: at n32 booked 585 s against 996 s reconstructed engine work: a 411 s (41 percent) under-count, of which 0b recovers only the part the belief KNEW was cold (to be measured by the 0b validation; the part where the belief said GPU but the engine had evicted stays unbooked); at n16 3:11:2 617 against 632 (no eviction: must book almost nothing extra).
**B, load-aware arrival admission (robot and single-shot) on D162 + 0b.** Trigger: queueing at the arrival gate: n32 (170 robot REJECTs with a median queue ahead of 18.8 s and 24 requests in flight; only 9 to 15 cold rebuilds would fit even with the measured queue), halved memory (34 robot declines at s4, 16 of which were served on time under `-q`). Mechanism: replace the isolated b7 estimate by the D162 first-token model on the uncached implied by the belief tier plus the queued uncached ahead (same comparator). Bound: B can only make admission accurate (fewer wrong rejects, fewer wrong admits), it cannot create capacity: halved s4 rung 3 FAIR share 0.674 (gap 22.0 units: 10.6 no request sent, 8.1 sent but late) with at most the 47 percent of declines that a warm-state arm served on time (16 of 34) as a ceiling for the admit side; n32 FAIR share 0.165 with the engine saturated: bound small; at n16 1x it must change nothing (no-harm).
**P, predictive belief (shadow cache from 0b bookings and the engine's eviction order up to the known KV capacity, giving a pre-send residency belief; no post-hoc inputs; the after-completion corrector unchanged).** Trigger: stale COLD belief after a decline or a zero-cached reading (the D261 latch): rung 3 halved s4: 34 declines, 21 repeats, residency said GPU for 10 and the engine held the prefix for 6 (cached 0.73 to 0.99); n32: 38 of 170 rejects had a warm signal (28 next requests at least 50 percent cached). Mechanism: replay the arm's own bookings through an LRU shadow of the engine cache so the belief tracks eviction without waiting for a completion. Bound: about 6 of 34 declines at halved s4 and 28 to 38 of 170 at n32 had a warm engine state; the unlatch arm shows the cost of the opposite error (9 cold late sends of 13 after an optimistic restore at halved s4): P must beat both.
**S, sync control (form-iv harm priced by D162 including the in-flight uncached work) on B + 0b.** Trigger: a correlated outage where the post-reconnect backlog drain overlaps the victims' first questions and rebuilds compete: at s4 rule A no victim was urgent (first question 38.3 s, drain 1 to 5 s): INCONCLUSIVE gate; at n32 k = 22, d = 188 about 7 victims ask within 30 s of reconnect and their summed cold rebuilds (2 to 3 s each) exceed the time to the last cold victim's question in 56 to 63 percent of t0 starts (gate 1; idle-gap relation unmeasured above 120 s). Mechanism: order the backlog releases and rebuilds by priority with harm priced on current queue and in-flight work. Bound: not measured yet; step 3 reports the unavoidable (asked during the outage; asked before any policy could have uploaded the needed history at the link rate) against the avoidable loss.
**CF, counterfactual admission and working-set protection (rung 4's protect-and-defer, plus deficit-rotated protection of a resident set that fits), on all of the above.** Trigger: working set above the cache with rebuild storms: n32 (33 evictions, 111 belief_to_cold, 405 s of unbooked re-prefill); the knee fleet. Mechanism: protect a resident subset that fits and defer the rest rather than thrash. Bound: the 405 s of engine work spent re-prefilling evicted prefixes at n32 (41 percent of engine work) is the upper bound on what protection could save; rung 4's protect-and-defer never acted in any committed cell (UNEXERCISED, not a result).
