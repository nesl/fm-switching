## What was built (inside the existing netting switch `live2_cf_s2_netting`; ON in F1, OFF everywhere else; -nonet keeps it OFF)
- THE RULE (symmetric, committed quantities only, no constant): a robot's future has ONE holder at a time. (1) S2 holds it from its first EXECUTED value release in a catch-up window (`State.first_rel`, set in `account()` for legal emitted sessions) until the catch-up COMPLETES = the committed window-close signal (`catchup_windows_closed_done` / `_closed_session_gone`, `State.first_rel` popped there); F1 may not credit that future while held: `CF.s2_claim_blocks(net_on, held, since, now)` is true when the claim was opened in an EARLIER epoch; F1's admission rests on FUTURE > 0, so a blocked admission is gate `future_claimed_by_s2` (the would-be net is logged on a `claim_block` row). (3) A claim opened in the SAME epoch goes to the existing higher-net-wins joint evaluation; when the admission wins, S2's release is removed AND its just-opened claim is cancelled (`first_rel`, `rel_dev`).
- OBSERVED COMPLETION (default `cf_claim_scope="window"`): the catch-up window close (the committed signal above). Alternative `scope="piece"` (arm `-cand-tl-sync-cf-claimpiece`, attribution only): held only while a piece/whole of the device is in flight (the W register, `obs.cloud.in_flight_work`). Both scopes are MEASURED on every admission (`cf3_claim_diag_window_would_block` / `_piece_would_block`); only the default scope acts.
