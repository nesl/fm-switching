## ONE SCALE (all committed; no new constant)
S2's net (catchup_value.evaluate): net_acc = gain_acc - harm_acc, fires iff > 0, in deficit-weighted expected on-time-correct-answer gain in FAIR accuracy units (on-time units x (ACC_QF - ACC_DEVICE) = 0.311067 for a robot; per-type harm by that type's gap). F1's admission (cf_forfeit.admit_decision) is in D177 on-time units: own = w_R p_self, FUTURE, HARM by type, fires iff own + FUTURE - HARM > bar (= w_R MARGIN_CF_ON_TIME). Put on S2's scale: adm_acc = (own + FUTURE - bar) x (ACC_QF - ACC_DEVICE) - sum_type harm_by_type[t] x gap[t] (the SAME `catchup_value._gaps()`; pending and arrival harm merged), i.e. each net is net of its OWN committed firing threshold (S2: 0; F1: the bar). Nothing missing, no STOP.
