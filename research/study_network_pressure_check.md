Backlog per victim = the trace's own `history_batch` records inside the window [t0, t0+d), `est_tokens` (90 tokens per frame, `frame_bytes` 24,570, both confirmed in the trace). Every t0 in 1.0 s steps with t0 + d + B <= 1200. Accepted starts: C1 1,006 (FAST), C2 1,002 (FAST), C3 890 (FAST); the SLOW and REBUILD counts are in the JSON (`n_accepted_starts`).

- FAST burst: total backlog / 17,945.2 tok/s (`oracle_lp_run.py:30`, D230 (12)).
- SLOW burst: sum over victims of `costs.recall_ttft_s(backlog_r, "COLD")` (`baselines/costs.py:106-121`). Note: the committed COLD rule uses the nearest-size rate (`COLD_PREFILL_TOK_S[_nearest_size]`, `costs.py:117-118`), not a linear interpolation between sizes; the check uses the committed rule.
- REBUILD (sensitivity, not a bound): each victim's full cumulative history at reconnect, rebuilt cold.
- Questions counted in [R, R+B), R = t0 + d: single-shot (asst devices), robot follow-ups (robo devices), dashcam frames (dash monitor opportunities).

## 5. KV demand (dtype finding)

- The committed H100 serve configuration `experiments/dashcam/sh_ep_server_h100.sh:10-16` passes `--dtype bfloat16` and no `--kv-cache-dtype`. The KV cache therefore runs at the model dtype, bf16 (2 bytes per element). The `fp8` KV flags found in the repo belong to other studies (`experiments/sember/study_rh1_history_ladder.py`, `experiments/cost/study_r5_fleet.py`), not to this serve path. The same file sets `--kv-offloading-size 27`, which matches the 27 GiB host tier.
- The committed constant `HOST_BYTES_PER_TOKEN = 262144` (`baselines/costs.py:90`) is 256 KiB per token. This matches bf16 for a model with 64 layers, 8 KV heads and head dimension 128 (K and V together). The model geometry is not recorded in any committed config, so the 256 KiB figure is confirmed as bf16 plus the committed constant, not as the geometry.

## 7. Positive control (proposed, not queued)

- Arm: **`always-cloud-fcfs`**. It sends every opportunity to the cloud first come, first served and holds nothing.
- Evidence: `experiments/orchestration/live2/arms.py:102-105` (class `AlwaysCloudFcfsArm`, "One request per opportunity, FCFS, no admission and no state management"), which points to `baselines/naive.py::AlwaysCloudFCFS` as committed.
- Why it is the eagerest committed sync: the sync hold is set only for arms that declare the switch on their own instance (`link_sync.py:50` SYNC_SWITCH, `:93-105` arm_enables, `:108-109` arm_controls_sync; `run_live2_real.py:401-404`, `:818`). No committed arm of the eager kind declares it, so `always-cloud-fcfs` releases the backlog as it arrives. The only arms that declare sync are `covex-v6-fair-final-q-sync` (priority release, `arms_v6cfr3.py:855-870`) and its FIFO control.
- Expected effect: single-shot on-time and dashcam within_L should drop against the paired control. This is the check the H100 cell pair would test.

## 8. Caveats

- The burst ignores vLLM chunked-prefill interleaving: short requests slow down rather than block. FAST and SLOW bracket the effect; neither is a bound on any policy.
- One seed (seed 4), one mix (3:11:2).
- Li et al. figures are per device; applying them to a whole site is the scenario's stated assumption.
- The FAST and SLOW bursts are computed from the trace's own backlog, not from a per-token estimate.

### R1.6 Caveats

- Revision 1 is a design. The bar and the exposure numbers are from the trace and throughput, not from a cell.
- The background-adjusted burst assumes the no-outage rung-3 cell's uncached rate is the competing load. It is one seed.
- The rebuild share takes the host tier as free for the backlog. The resident amount depends on other sessions' KV, which is not modelled here.
- Placement by top-10 exposure selects the high-dose starts by construction.

### R2.6 B_obs calibration (group A, rung 3 n32, k22, d188, t0 529.5): FAILED as a calibration

- Predicted (capacity model, the same fleet, 7,860.2 tok/s): **B = 81.7 s**, with backlog 185,856 tokens.
- Observed, sustained rule (first start after R from which every 30 s window has the group-A uncached mean <= 1.10 x the n32 control mean): **B_obs = 260 s**.
- Cumulative uncached excess of group A over the control in the 300 s after R: **-35,038 tokens** (group A carries less uncached prefill than the control there, not more).
- Interpretation: the backlog drain is not visible as extra uncached prefill in the committed cell. Either the backlog is prefilled with cache hits (so it does not appear in uncached tokens) or the relief from the outage dominates. The burst model is therefore **unvalidated**. Designs based on it (B = 19 to 29 s here) are conditional on this gap and should not be read as calibrated.

### R2.7 Caveats

- The design uses B_cap and B_own, not an observed burst. Under the B_obs failure above, these are model predictions only.
- Top-10 ranking selects maximum dose by construction (see R1.4).
- One seed; the n16 control is pre-D249.
