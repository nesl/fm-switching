## C1 — Fidelity of a state representation is workload-dependent

**Status: established**

For some workloads, full-replay is strictly necessary; for others, summary-80 is
statistically equivalent. The gap is large, consistent, and crosses models.

| Workload | full | summary-80 | gap | p | regime |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LoCoMo (n=282, cat=1) | 34.0% | 9.9% | −24.1pp | <0.001 | dense-incompressible |
| LoCoMo (n=100, Mistral-7B) | — | — | large (matched) | <0.05 | dense-incompressible |
| EgoSchema (n=500, Qwen2.5-7B) | 50.2% | 47.0% | −3.2pp | ns | gist-compressible |
| Infini-THOR (n=57 non-salient, Qwen2.5-7B) | — | — | +9.8pp | 0.211 | structured-compressible |

Evidence: `results/fidelity/locomo_audit_scaled_qwen7b.json`,
`results/frontier_qwen7b.json`, `results/fidelity/multimodel/locomo_qwen7b_n100.json`,
`reports/phase0a_multimodel_audit.md`.

## C2 — Semantic inertia depends on the reader model

**Status: tentative**

Infini-THOR under Mistral-7B shows full>>summary at n=57 non-salient (gap +0.073,
p=0.441; all-pool gap +0.140, p=0.033), while Qwen2.5-7B shows summary≈full (+0.098,
p=0.211). However, Mistral approaches floor accuracy (near-chance), so the apparent
reader effect may be a floor artifact. The cross-summarizer decomposition (summary
generated by model A, read by model B) reveals a reader effect but the confound is
not yet disentangled from absolute-accuracy differences.

Requires: a second dense-incompressible workload where both models are above floor.

Evidence: `reports/phase0a_multimodel_audit.md`, §Infini-THOR, Truncation Handling.

## C3 — Structured or coded physical logs compress; heterogeneous free-form state does not

**Status: tentative**

Infini-THOR trajectory logs (873–2,149 tokens, templated oracle format) are
structured-compressible: summary-80 ≈ full under Qwen.
GeoLife traces (251–728 token contexts) pass the micro-gate for incompressibility
(full=58.5%, s80=22.0%, gap +36.6pp, p<0.0001) but the question family (sequential
recall) may be look-up type with guessable priors — GeoLife is archived.
Oxford RobotCar and sensor-stream feasibility checks showed context token counts of
~6–40K and ~0.5–2K respectively; both are feasible inputs but neither has a QA
benchmark that isolates compression as the failure mode.

Evidence: `results/fidelity/frontier_infinithor_qwen7b.json`,
`results/archive/geolife_microgate_qwen7b.json`,
`reports/oxford_av_feasibility.md`, `reports/sensor_stream_feasibility.md`.

## C4 — Physical inertia is large and grows with context length; warm-append vs cold re-prefill ratio ~68× at 64K on A6000; 3090 Ti OOM above 32K

**Status: established**

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 5 reps per L-point (first excluded as warm-up):

| L | full-restore (A6000) | incr-warm (A6000) | cold/warm ratio | 3090Ti feasible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1K | 165 ms | 66 ms | 3× | yes |
| 8K | 1,369 ms | 94 ms | 15× | yes |
| 32K | 7,805 ms | 115 ms | 68× | yes (last feasible) |
| 64K | 21,720 ms | 330 ms | 68× | OOM at 49K+ |

Sum-80 update (corrected, full L-token input): scales linearly with L, ~16s at 64K.
Sum-80 restore: ~28ms constant (fixed 80-token stub).
xB (summary pipeline < full re-prefill): A6000 ≈ 65K; 3090Ti = never in range
(update OOMs at L≥32K before crossover).
xC (window ≥10× cheaper): A6000 = 8K; 3090Ti = 4K.
xA (re-prefill < text transfer): only at 200ms RTT; RTT-dominated, not bandwidth-limited.

Evidence: `results/cost/profiles/a6000_qwen7b.json`,
`results/cost/profiles/rtx3090ti_qwen7b.json`,
`results/cost/cost_matrix.csv`, `reports/phase1_cost_profiling.md`.

## C6 — Context Inertia is decision-relevant for single-session edge inference on EgoSchema

**Status: rejected**

The RQ3 ablation (`rq3_ablation.json`) shows that representation choice does not affect
placement or latency decisions on EgoSchema because the workload is gist-compressible:
quality(summary-80) ≈ quality(full), so there is no quality SLO that a representation
change could violate. The claim was correct only for dense-incompressible workloads.

Evidence: `results/orchestration/rq3_ablation.json`.
