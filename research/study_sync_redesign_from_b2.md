# SYNC REDESIGN FROM THE B2 MECHANISM (2026-11-26T21:26:12Z): THREE LINES AND DESIGN ONLY. PROVISIONAL; NOT BUILT; build after P, 0b and B (dependency order); switches default OFF; light checks per build; nothing recorded as a design decision. Evidence: research/study_b2_network_bound_outage_gate.md (readout and follow-up).
## THREE LINES
**Trigger (deployment condition):** a correlated outage whose reconnect backlog drains on a slow per-device uplink: at the sourced p10 uplink (2.219 Mbps, Ookla, flagged proxy) and a mean outage (188 s, Li et al., per-device) the drain (about 8.3 s per victim, 94 frames) overlaps at least 2 victims' first questions in 45 percent of placements; B2 cell n16 3:11:2, k = 11, t0 535.5.
**Mechanism:** (a) QUESTION TEXT BYPASSES THE FRAME BACKLOG in the device's uplink queue (the 1,200 B question text is sent ahead of the queued history frames instead of behind them in the per-device FIFO); (b) CATCH-UP PREFILL of the backlog delta after reconnect (the frames already uploaded are prefilled in advance, paced by GPU headroom and the predicted next-question time, ordered by deficit), so a later question finds a prefix that is already resident instead of carrying a 5k to 15k-token delta that must be prefilled inside its own deadline.
**Recoverable bound (B2, one seed, one placement):** from the B2 readout: the 2 in-drain questions that EXPIRED behind the backlog (robo001 asked 723.858 and robo005 asked 726.267: text arrived 5.06 s and 2.65 s past the 3 s deadline; no decision, device answer stood) and the 1 in-drain question served late on service time (4.64 s; cached 5,040 of 12,112); the 5 questions lost in the first 30 s after the drain (4 with a large uncached delta of 5k to 15k tokens, 1 a GPU queue; upload wait 0 s so (a) does not help them: they need (b)); the post-reconnect dashcam dip (first 60 s after reconnect 0.497 against 0.804 in the control, 5 of 6 later 60 s windows negative; the part of it caused by the +11.7 robot GPU-s burst, not separated from seed noise); **at most 8 of the 35 lost cloud on-time robot asks (about 3.5 percent of 229), of which 17 asked during the outage are unavoidable; against ACTUAL device answers the 3 in-drain questions would not gain cbd (the device answered them correctly): the bound is in cloud on-time asks and dashcam, not in cbd.**
## DESIGN (not built)
**(a) Question-text bypass.** Where: the emulator's per-device uplink (fleet/links.py `UplinkQueue` :104, FIFO by offer time :112; emulator.py `_upload` :488 and question upload :557 which share one queue per device). Change: two classes on the SAME device queue: class 0 (question text, 1,200 B) is served ahead of class 1 (history frames) at the next dispatch opportunity (non-preemptive: a frame already on the wire completes; at 24,570 B per 2.219 Mbps = 0.0886 s that bounds the wait to about 0.09 s); class order for equal class stays FIFO. Scope: this is DEVICE-SIDE scheduling (the robot's own uplink scheduler), implemented in the emulator link model behind a switch (`--uplink-question-priority`, default OFF, driver token needed; the user's approval of new code per switch applies). Check: with the switch OFF all existing outputs byte-identical; with it ON on the B2 outage dry replay the two expiring questions' upload wait falls from 8.06 and 5.65 s to under 0.1 s and they reach the coordinator before their deadlines (then B / the arrival gate decides on the cold-or-warm state).
**(b) Catch-up prefill, paced.** Where: the coordinator's piece path (`_advance_build` :1489, `MATERIALIZE` in `act.sessions`, the 46 s per-session slot (arms_v6state.decide `drop` set ~:868-926), `can_spend`/`_share_ok`/W register) and the link events (link_events.json: reconnect and arrival times; `link_read.py` `device_links` observation). Trigger: at a victim's reconnect (or when its backlog frames arrive: arrival times known from the link events) the session has a delta of D frames (about 90 tokens per frame) that no earlier request carried; a CATCH-UP PIECE request prefills the delta (extending the cached prefix; the same request shape as a piece). Pacing (no new constant if possible; escalate otherwise): (1) GPU HEADROOM: release a catch-up chunk only when the D162 price of the chunk (0.00018243 s x uncached, plus the in-flight register W) fits the headroom the booked-work register shows (the same headroom quantity the alert re-check uses); chunk size = the existing piece size (about 1,024 tokens, WARM_AHEAD_SLICE_TOKENS) so the interference per chunk is the committed step-model value (0.092 s); (2) PREDICTED NEXT-QUESTION TIME: the arm already estimates `next_question_s = _last_q_t + _gap_ewma` per device (arms_v6state `_p_question`): order sessions by (predicted question time minus the time to finish their catch-up at the paced rate), earliest-deadline first, i.e. the session whose question is most likely soon is caught up first; (3) DEFICIT ORDER: ties and the gain weight use the deficit weight `counters.weight` (w_d, D185) as for every other harm term; (4) it must price its own harm to the dashcam rows through the existing booked-work register (0b-from-P corrected booking: a catch-up chunk is a piece and is booked as one) so it does not recreate the +11.7 GPU-s burst at question time: it MOVES the prefill earlier and spreads it over the 188-plus seconds of idle GPU headroom before most victims' first question (first-question gaps after reconnect: 0.4 to 415 s). Interaction: needs P (to know what is already cached: P's shadow tells a catch-up chunk is needed) and 0b-from-P (booking), B (arrival gate then sees a larger cached fraction and admits), and S's backlog release ordering; switch `live2_catchup_prefill` default OFF. Check: OFF byte-identical; ON on the outage dry replay: catch-up pieces released after reconnect (count > 0), first differing decision, and the first question after reconnect for a victim carries a smaller uncached delta than in rung 3 (the P replay on the B2 cell predicts the fraction).
**Validation plan (offline first, no cells):** replay the B2 outage cell's own stream with the catch-up schedule inserted (booked as pieces; the engine work conserved: prefill tokens moved earlier, not added) and report, per victim, the uncached delta at its first question with and without (the P shadow gives the prediction), the GPU-s headroom used, and the predicted dashcam interference from the step model (0.092 s per 1,024-token chunk; no fitted constant); a GPU cell only after the user approves.
