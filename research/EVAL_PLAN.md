### Definitional note on "the same 6:7:3 proportions"

6:7:3 is what the committed ratio **40:40:20** rounds to at 16 devices (`fleet/trace.py:_split_fleet3`, dashcam and
assistive rounded, robot the remainder). No other fleet size in the plan can carry exactly 6:7:3. The plan therefore
holds the **ratio** fixed at 40:40:20, which is also what the queued LOAD1220 cells and every committed load trace use:

| devices | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dashcam : robot : assistive | 3 : 3 : 2 | 5 : 5 : 2 | 6 : 7 : 3 | 8 : 8 : 4 | 10 : 9 : 5 |

The composition conditions do round exactly at 16 devices: 10:4:2 and 3:11:2.

## Status rules

- An arm with an open fidelity hit waits at **every** condition until its fix is proven, because the fix changes its
  decision code and a 1x result is not safe either. The exception is a card check showing the arm never reads the
  field. Open hits: `simplestream` 14 and 7; `llf` and `mooncake` 14 (card check pending); `eco-llm` 14 and 16.
- Cells already landed for those arms are `done (provisional)` until the fix is proven. The four landed 0.25x cells of
  the hit-14 arms are SUPERSEDED.
- `confidence-cascade` (fixed under D205), `covex-v6-base` and `covex-v6-rules` (they read no field with an open hit)
  are ready wherever the condition is built.
- Still held: 0.25x policy cells (until rung 4 is proven and the user approves), ablations (D210), KV025_RUNGS, and
  the hit-14-affected 0.25x baselines.

## Identical conditions across arms

The harness generates each trace once per directory key (`run_live2_real.py:188-222`: seed, devices, ratio, run
length, mode, question sets, deadline, dashcam format, upload mode, re-ask mean) and every later arm at that key
reuses it unchanged. So every arm at one seed and condition sees the same trace. Each trace's digests (sha256 of
`trace.jsonl` and `device_answers.jsonl`, plus the trace's own `fairness_digest`) are recorded once in
`results/orchestration/live2/frozen_test/trace_digests.jsonl`. The per-run loop (`proof_load_loop.sh`) appends each
newly generated trace after its first cell and commits it. Cells also carry `digests_equal` in `cell.json`.
